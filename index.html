Our mission

We are building an AI assistant that helps older adults, and anyone who finds technology hard, complete high-stakes applications: Medicare and Medicaid renewals, benefits applications, and hospice and caregiver paperwork.

A missed field or a wrong answer on these forms can mean delayed care or lost benefits. Our goal is to make getting them right simple, patient, and safe.

How it works

  • Only the facts you give it. The assistant fills in only what the person has told it or what their own documents show. It never guesses.
  • Plain language, one step at a time. Questions are asked simply, at the person's pace, with large, readable text.
  • Understanding comes first. Before anything is submitted, the assistant checks that the person understands what the form says and what they are agreeing to.
  • Caregivers can help. Family members and facility staff can review alongside the person.

Where we're starting

We are at the concept stage. Our plan is to pilot with hospice and nursing-home facilities across Louisiana first, learn from residents and staff, and then expand nationally.
